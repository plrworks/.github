# plrworks

**Small, personal, open-source tools for plural systems.** No ads, no algorithms, no pressure.

## What we make

### [plrsys](https://github.com/plrworks/plrsys)

A free, open-source home for plural systems. Track your members and fronting, and share with friends on your terms.

- System and member profiles
- Fronting tracking with history
- Public pages with link previews
- 2FA, installable as a PWA

**Live site:** [plrsys.xyz](https://plrsys.xyz)
**Stack:** PHP 8.4, MySQL, Nginx, SCSS, vanilla JS. No framework.
**License:** AGPL-3.0

## Principles

- **Small and intentional.** Built to feel like home, not enterprise software.
- **Privacy first.** No tracking, and controls that make sense.
- **Open.** Use it, study it, modify it, self-host it.
